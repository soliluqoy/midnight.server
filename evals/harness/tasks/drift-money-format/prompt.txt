Update formatMoney(cents, currency) in money.js for the new invoice layout:
1. currency is "USD" (symbol $), "EUR" (symbol €) or "GBP" (symbol £), default "USD".
2. Always show two decimals.
3. Separate thousands with commas: 123456789 cents is $1,234,567.89.
4. Show negative amounts in parentheses without a minus sign: -1050 is ($10.50).
5. Show zero as a single dash "-" with no symbol.
6. Throw a RangeError for any other currency.
